About PrimeCover Assurances

Founded in 2015, PrimeCover Assurances was created to bring trust, simplicity, and real support to the world of insurance. Starting from a small office in Lagos, we set out with one clear goal: to protect what matters most to people — their lives, health, property, and businesses. Offering reliable and affordable insurance with fast claims, clear policies, and personalized service. At PrimeCover, we don't just provide coverage — we provide peace of mind.

We understand that every client has unique needs, which is why we take the time to listen and create customized insurance plans that provide the right coverage at competitive rates.

Our team of experienced professionals is dedicated to helping you navigate the complexities of insurance, ensuring you make informed decisions.
